Refund Policy - TravelWithEV.com

Last Updated: 04 May 2026

Thank you for choosing TravelWithEV.com for your EV rental, EV travel, charging-related services, and EV tour booking needs. We aim to provide a reliable and convenient electric vehicle travel experience for our customers.

This Refund Policy explains how cancellations, refunds, and booking changes are handled when you make a booking or payment through TravelWithEV.com.

  1. Booking Cancellation by Customer
    Customers may request to cancel an EV rental booking by contacting our customer support team. Cancellation eligibility depends on the time of cancellation and the booking conditions.
    • Cancellation made more than 48 hours before pickup: Eligible for a refund after deducting any applicable processing or administrative charges.
    • Cancellation made within 24 to 48 hours before pickup: Partial refund may be provided depending on vehicle availability, partner charges, and booking conditions.
    • Cancellation made less than 24 hours before pickup: Refund may not be available.
  2. No-Show Policy
    If the customer does not arrive at the scheduled pickup location and does not inform TravelWithEV.com in advance, the booking will be considered a no-show. In such cases, the payment may be non-refundable.
  3. Booking Changes
    Customers may request to change pickup time, drop-off time, vehicle type, or travel dates. Changes are subject to EV availability, partner availability, price difference, route or location availability, and operational approval by TravelWithEV.com. Additional charges may apply.
  4. Refunds for Service Unavailability
    If TravelWithEV.com is unable to provide the booked EV due to vehicle unavailability, technical issues, or operational reasons, we may offer:
    • A replacement EV
    • A new booking date
    • A partial refund
    • A full refund, where applicable
  5. Vehicle Condition and Damage
    Customers are responsible for using the rented EV carefully and returning it in good condition. Refunds will not be provided if the customer causes:
    • Vehicle damage
    • Loss of vehicle accessories
    • Late return charges
    • Cleaning charges
    • Traffic fines or legal penalties
    • Misuse of the vehicle
    • Violation of rental conditions
  6. Charging, Range, and Travel Responsibility
    TravelWithEV.com may provide EV journey planning assistance, charging information, or route guidance. However, EV range may vary depending on driving style, road conditions, weather, passenger load, vehicle condition, and charging availability. Refunds will not be provided for issues caused by customer misuse, ignoring charging guidance, or route changes made by the customer.
  7. Refund Processing Time
    Approved refunds will be processed within 7 to 14 business days after approval. Refunds will be made to the original payment method where possible. The time taken for the refund to appear in the customer’s account may depend on the bank, card provider, or payment gateway.
  8. Payment Gateway Charges
    Any payment gateway charges, bank charges, card processing fees, or administrative fees may be deducted from the refund amount where applicable.
  9. Non-Refundable Items
    The following may be non-refundable:
    • Booking confirmation fees
    • Last-minute cancellations
    • No-show bookings
    • Used rental time
    • Completed services
    • Special tour package reservations
    • Partner hotel or activity reservation charges
    • Payment gateway or bank processing charges
